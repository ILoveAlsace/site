Château Lacour

A five-minute walk from central Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines, Château Lacour offers spacious apartments in an early 20th-century residence connected with the valley’s textile history. Its garden, private parking and generous rooms make it a distinctive base for families and groups of friends.

Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines
