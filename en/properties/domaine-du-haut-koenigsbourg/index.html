Domaine du Haut-Koenigsbourg

Located on the road to Haut-Koenigsbourg, the Domaine offers rooms with private bathrooms, a spacious breakfast room and a terrace overlooking the landscape. The emphasis is on simplicity, calm and immediate proximity to the castle.

Location

Orschwiller

The Domaine is in Orschwiller, on the forested heights leading to Haut-Koenigsbourg. The castle is about 1.8 km away, while the Alsace plain, Sélestat, Colmar and the Wine Route are easily reached by car.
